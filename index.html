📋 Challenge brief — approved hooks & content guardrails

Approved hooks — feature at least one per video

"If you struggle with ___ like me, you need to try this moisturizer" (dryness, sensitive skin, damaged skin barrier)
"There's a reason this product has won 8x prestige beauty awards and has over 1k 5-star reviews…"
"I'm picky about moisturizers because of my sensitive skin, and THIS passed the test."
"This is your PSA to switch to this rich, barrier-building Skinfix moisturizer NOW so your skin barrier doesn't suffer in the colder fall/winter months"

Visual requirement: side-by-side or transition-cut before/after in every video.

Content guardrails

✓ Do's
  • Shoot all content vertically
  • Shoot in well-lit, optimal lighting — natural light when possible
  • Have clean nails
  • Show the product in the first 0–2 seconds
  • Keep length between 15–35 seconds
  • Start with a strong HOOK and end with a CTA
  • Show a packaging close-up with the name clearly visible (not inverted on camera)
  • Show texture and application on real skin
  • Include text overlay or closed captions highlighting product benefits and ingredient callouts
✕ Don'ts
  • Use any filters
  • Use any tools
  • Show other brands or trademarks
  • Show excess skin or cleavage, or wear distracting clothing
  • Use copyrighted music
  • Show a messy background or product packaging
